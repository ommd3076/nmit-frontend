# Anti-Slop Frontend Craft

## Hard Anti-Patterns (NEVER DO)
1. **Generic AI Layouts**: Centered headlines over 3 lines with floating pastel blobs, generic icon cards, and vague marketing copy.
2. **Invented Numbers & Fake Badges**: No dummy statistics or fabricated data. All data must come from real simulation or backend domain outputs.
3. **Empty Restraint**: Taking ornaments away without adding substance. Substance means confident typography, purposeful data tables, live maps, real routes, and explicit evidence.
4. **Indiscriminate Gradients & Borders**: No purple-to-pink gradient overlays on everything. Use crisp glass surfaces, frosted backdrops, and intentional contrast.
5. **No Placeholders**: Never ship empty states that say "Lorem ipsum" or non-functional buttons.
